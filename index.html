📌 About KJSEA

The Kenya Junior School Education Assessment (KJSEA) is taken by Grade 9 learners under the CBC curriculum. The assessment covers learning outcomes from Grade 7, Grade 8 and Grade 9.

Subjects include: Mathematics, English, Kiswahili, Integrated Science, Social Studies, Pre-Technical Studies, Agriculture, Creative Arts, Religious Education, Business Studies and Computer Studies.
